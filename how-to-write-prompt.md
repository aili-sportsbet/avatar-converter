Commonly used prompts for AI portraits and professional photography, categorized by their specific functions:

### 1. Base Prompts and Realism Constraints

A strong base prompt acts as a blueprint, combining technical settings with high-level descriptions of the subject.

* 
**Formula Structure**: Subject + Pose + Clothing + Background + Lighting + Realism Constraints.


* 
**Realism Directives**: "Observe crisp detail" to prevent waxy or over-smoothed skin.


* 
**Technical Modifiers**: "85mm f/1.8 lens" to minimize facial distortion and provide soft bokeh.


* 
**Quality Keywords**: "Ultra-realistic studio portrait," "high-resolution," "sharp focus," and "refined film grain" for professional texture.


* 
**Identity Preservation**: Phrases like "maintaining the exact facial structure, identity, and key features of the person in the input image" are foundational for consistent headshots.



### 2. Facial Features and Makeup

Prompts for the face often focus on natural textures and subtle enhancements rather than over-processed looks.

* 
**Skin Texture**: "Natural skin texture with visible pores," "dewy skin finish," and "subtle highlights on cheekbones".


* 
**Eyes and Expression**: "Subtle catchlights in the eyes," "warm and radiant smile," "confident, authoritative expression," or a "soft, gentle, and slightly playful" look.


* **Makeup Styles**:
* 
**Natural**: "Polished yet natural," "soft peach blush," and "nude or soft pink lipstick with satin finish".


* 
**Editorial**: "Matte lip glaze," "earth-tone eyeshadow," and "long eyelashes".





### 3. Hair Styles

Hair descriptions help define the "vibe" of the portrait, from corporate to casual.

* 
**Corporate/Professional**: "Sleek black turtleneck with hair in soft waves," "dark long hair tied up," or "neatly styled for a studio shoot".


* 
**Natural/Artistic**: "Messy black short hair with a few strands across the face," "natural hair flow," and "soft hair at the temples".



### 4. Clothing and Fashion Styles

Clothing prompts establish the professional context and color harmony of the image.

* 
**Professional/Corporate**: "Premium navy business suit," "beige tailored suit," "crisp silk linen white shirt," or "sleek black turtleneck".


* 
**Modern/High-Fashion**: "Avant-garde gown," "sharply tailored asymmetrical blazer dress in emerald green," or "minimalist black suit with structured shoulders".


* 
**Accessories**: "Delicate blue printed silk scarf tied neatly inside the collar" or "elegant gold stud earrings".



### 5. Poses and Gestures (Framing)

Proper framing and posing prevent common AI errors like awkward cropping.

* 
**Framing**: "Chest-up framing" with "ample headroom and negative space above the head" ensures the top of the head isn't cut off.


* 
**Angles**: "Three-quarter profile angle," "head slightly turned toward the camera," or "subject's body positioned at a slight 3/4 angle".


* 
**Stance**: "Statuesque and elegant" while seated, or "captured mid-stride as if on a catwalk" for runway-inspired shots.



### 6. Lighting and Backgrounds

Lighting and background set the tone, whether it is for finance (trust) or creative fields (innovation).

* **Lighting Types**:
* 
**Studio**: "Soft directional lighting," "bright, even front lighting," or "single overhead spotlight for artistic shadows".


* 
**Natural**: "Natural soft lighting" or "autumn atmosphere".




* **Background Choices**:
* 
**Neutral/Professional**: "Light gray or off-white smooth gradient," "near-black neutral gray (#141414)," or "clean white for official ID photos".


* 
**Industry-Specific**: "Navy blue" for finance/law (trust) and "charcoal" for executive authority.





### 7. Negative Prompts

Negative prompts are critical for preventing artifacts and maintaining realism.

* 
**Common Exclusions**: "Extra fingers," "warped faces," "unnaturally smooth skin," and "AI artifacts".


* 
**Object Prevention**: "Ensure no phones or handheld devices are visible".


* 
**Quality Control**: Avoiding "blurry," "low-quality," "plastic-looking," or "stock photo" appearances.
